================================================================================
  CONVENIENCE STORE POS — QUICK USER GUIDE
  (One-page reference for store owner and cashiers)
================================================================================


OPENING THE SYSTEM EACH DAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open XAMPP → Start Apache and MySQL (both green).
  2. Open Chrome or Edge.
  3. Go to:  http://localhost/pos_system/
  4. Log in with your username and password.


TWO TYPES OF USERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ADMIN (Owner/Manager)              CASHIER (Staff)
  -------------------------          -------------------------
  Dashboard — today's summary        Cashier — ring up sales
  Products — add/edit items          (Only sees Cashier screen)
  Categories — organize products
  Users — manage staff accounts
  Sales — view past transactions
  Reports — sales & inventory reports
  Settings — store name on receipt


HOW TO MAKE A SALE (CASHIER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Log in → you arrive at the Cashier screen.
  2. The barcode box is ready — scan a product (or type barcode + Enter).
  3. The item appears in the cart on the left.
  4. Scan more items as needed.
     - Same item scanned twice? Quantity increases automatically.
     - Wrong item? Click the trash icon to remove it.
     - Need more/less? Use the + and - buttons.
  5. On the right side:
     - Check the TOTAL amount.
     - Apply discount if needed (₱ amount or %).
     - Type how much cash the customer gave (Cash Tendered).
     - Check the CHANGE shown.
  6. Click CHECKOUT.
  7. Receipt prints automatically — give it to the customer.
  8. Click "New Sale" to serve the next customer.

  To cancel everything: click "Clear Cart".


ADDING A NEW PRODUCT (ADMIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Go to Products in the sidebar.
  2. Click "Add Product".
  3. Fill in:
     - Barcode  (the number on the package — must be unique)
     - Name     (e.g. Coca-Cola 1.5L)
     - Category
     - Price    (selling price in pesos)
     - Cost     (what you paid — optional, for your records)
     - Stock    (how many you have on hand)
     - Unit     (pcs, bottle, pack, sachet, etc.)
  4. Click "Save Product".

  RESTOCKING:
  - Click "Bulk Stock" on the Products page.
  - Enter positive numbers to add stock (e.g. +24 for a new case).
  - Click "Save Adjustments".


CHECKING SALES & REPORTS (ADMIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SALES page:
  - See every transaction with date, OR number, cashier, and total.
  - Filter by date range.
  - Click the receipt icon to reprint any sale.

  REPORTS page:
  - Total revenue and transactions for a date range.
  - Top selling products.
  - Sales per cashier.
  - Low stock items that need reordering.
  - Print or export to CSV.


LOW STOCK ALERTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Products with stock below 5 (default) show a warning on the Dashboard.
  - Cashiers see a "Low Stock" badge when scanning low items.
  - Check Reports → Low Stock Report for the full list.


RECEIPT INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Each receipt shows:
  - Your store name, address, and TIN
  - OR number (e.g. OR-20250606-0001)
  - Date, time, and cashier name
  - List of items with prices
  - Subtotal, discount (if any), total
  - Cash tendered and change
  - Thank-you message at the bottom

  Update store details anytime under Settings.


TIPS FOR SMOOTH OPERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ ] Keep XAMPP Apache and MySQL running during store hours.
  [ ] Always focus on the barcode box before scanning.
  [ ] Count cash tendered carefully — system computes change for you.
  [ ] Restock products when Dashboard shows low stock alerts.
  [ ] Back up the database weekly (see INSTALL.txt).
  [ ] Do not share the admin password — create cashier accounts instead.


COMMON QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Q: Scanner not working?
  A: Click inside the barcode box first, then scan. Check USB connection.

  Q: Receipt not printing?
  A: Make sure the thermal printer is on and set as default in Windows.

  Q: Product not found when scanning?
  A: The barcode may not be in the system — add it under Products (admin).

  Q: Wrong price on receipt?
  A: Edit the product under Products. New price applies to future sales.

  Q: Internet needed?
  A: No. The system runs entirely on your computer (offline/local).


QUICK REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  System URL:     http://localhost/pos_system/
  Default admin:  admin / admin123  (change after setup!)
  Start server:   XAMPP → Apache + MySQL ON
  Make a sale:    Cashier → Scan → Cash → Checkout
  Add product:    Products → Add Product
  View sales:     Sales (filter by date)
  Store info:     Settings
